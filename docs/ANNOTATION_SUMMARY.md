# Annotation scheme (short)

For each Owo word that differs from its Standard Yorùbá counterpart:

- **type**: Tonal (same letters, different tone marks) · Phonological (letters change by a recurring sound rule) ·
  Lexical (a different word) · Loanword (borrowed from English)
- **std_word**: the Standard word it replaces
- **pattern**: the named change (for example `w → gh`, `-in → -ẹn`, `final letter → n`)

Sentences with no differences have no rows in `annotations.csv` (`n_diffs = 0`). Full details are in the workbook
(`Owo_Yoruba_Difference_Workbook_v3_3.xlsx`, Guide and Patterns tabs).
